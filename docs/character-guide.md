# Blood Emblem - Character Guide

Companion to the [story bible](story-bible.md). This is author-facing documentation, not text to display as character biographies during play.

## Status and scope

This guide covers all **twelve existing playable characters**, followed by a supporting-cast and antagonist reference. Generic enemy profiles are roles or populations, not eighty-six separate people requiring invented biographies.

- **Existing foundation** means something present in the demo's profiles, equipment, recruitment or story copy. Existing dialogue is evidence of prior characterization, not approved presentation.
- **Proposed background and arc** means a recommendation, not newly established canon. All detailed histories, faction assignments beyond the existing foundation, private knowledge and late-game commitments below remain proposals.
- The [story bible](story-bible.md) takes precedence: no fate, no chosen bearer, no spoken explanations, and no secret replacement contract for Maria.
- Lantern Survey and Sanguine Covenant remain working names. Using either here does not finalize the name or implement Covenant promotions.
- No gameplay, recruitment conditions, classes, item effects, dialogue or saves change through this document.

## 1. Why a company forms

The party is not twelve strangers who all immediately agree to defeat an ancient god. Their goals converge in stages.

1. **Survive the same immediate threat.** The ship, village, prison and infirmary create practical cooperation.
2. **Observe reliability.** Maria protects people while looking for Rictor. Rictor helps others while escaping confinement. Neither demands unquestioning personal loyalty.
3. **Discover that the threat is connected.** Coastal raids, quarantine, treatment shipments and the underground route form one operation.
4. **Reach a real decision at the Bell Lift.** Finding Rictor and defeating Voss remove the original reasons for several allies to continue. Visible evidence must supply new ones before descent.
5. **Choose a limited common purpose.** Stop the ongoing operation and reach its source. This does not require identical beliefs about what will be found or how it should end.

### Why not simply escape?

Leaving is a real alternative, not a foolish thought forbidden by the plot. The sea is dangerous, but it is not a magical wall that conveniently prevents departure until the last chapter. A capable survivor might leave.

The company descends because the active system continues to threaten people each character values, including people beyond the island if distribution continues. They have seen a specific route and have the means to interrupt it. Their decision is informed but incomplete; none can promise a cure or a safe return.

Do not strand every ally with a destroyed boat, kill every loved one, or reveal everyone is infected. Those shortcuts make commitment indistinguishable from having no choice.

### Silent commitment

At Chapter 16, show party members preparing for the lift: tightening a shield strap, setting aside an escape chart, packing ordinary medicines, comparing a shipment tag, or carrying a tool previously used to open cages. Then show the company boarding.

These are recommended staging beats, not a mandatory montage for every character. The player retains tactical control. No loyalty meter, forced deployment, private cutscene or new survival condition is implied.

## 3. Individual character dossiers

### Maria - A search that becomes a choice

**Existing foundation:** Rictor's wife, travelling to Mournhollow after his disappearance. She starts as a Hunter with a Rapier he gave her and carries the Blood Emblem. The Rapier and Vigil Lance share a half-filed crest.

**Proposed background:** a practiced civilian hunter and traveller, not a concealed noble, ordained slayer or military prodigy. Rictor supplied a dependable sword, not her entire capacity for action. Her experience reading tracks, managing scarce supplies and defending herself makes the journey plausible without giving her prior knowledge of the plague.

**Faction:** independent. Marriage to an Order member is not membership; possession of the Blood Emblem is not consent to its contract.

**Why she leads and why others stay:** her search gives the surface party a destination, but her conduct gives it a reason to cooperate. The village escape, Linus's position and civilian rescues establish that she does not treat everyone else as expendable obstacles to finding Rictor.

**Why go deeper after finding him:** this is her essential turn. At the freight yard, reunion occurs beside people still being transported. Taking Rictor home would complete her private search, not end the danger they have uncovered. She chooses to help interrupt that operation because she has seen its victims, not because the emblem commands her.

**Knowledge boundary:** she knows Rictor and his belongings, not his whole institutional history. She learns the emblem's provenance with the player. She has no innate comprehension of the Deep One.

**Story through play:** a mobile sword user initially needs Bartz's help against spears; the company is useful to her, not merely dependent on her. Her later Ravager tools express learned brutality against beasts. Optional half-damage healing from the equipped Blood Emblem presents a troubling means of survival, not a scripted moral verdict on using the build.

**Evidence and friction:** paired weapon repairs show the marriage; different treatment of civilian and military spaces shows her choices. The emblem wrapper can establish Rictor's trust and his failure to explain the risk. Do not frame her as grateful to have been kept uninformed.

**Arc:** recover one person -> recognize the system threatening many -> choose to intervene without becoming its replacement ruler.

### Bartz - The pirate who already knows the water

**Existing foundation:** a pirate leader who boards Maria's ship, is defeated, and fights beside her when Merfolk attack. He carries an Iron Axe and has strong HP and Strength. Axes have weapon-triangle advantage against the Merfolk's spears.

**Proposed background:** before leading a raiding crew, Bartz worked the island's coastal freight runs. He learned reefs, tides, inspection points and which shipments were worth guarding. Requisitions, closed harbors and increasingly dangerous crossings destroyed legitimate work. He turned to smuggling and then piracy, choosing profit and taking it from other people; necessity does not make those choices harmless.

He has fought Merfolk repeatedly while boarding wrecks, recovering cargo and defending small boats. He knows how they climb, how their long spears catch in rigging, and when they surface around grounded vessels. He is not secretly a scholar, an ancient survivor or a heroic admiral.

**Faction:** independent pirate, formerly a freight hand or hired deck fighter. Some of his prizes came from the Church's routes; that does not make him a sworn Covenant member.

**Why attack the ship:** provisions and saleable cargo, possibly a familiar protected shipping mark. This remains opportunistic piracy. He did not stage the attack to test Maria or retrieve her emblem.

**Why stay with Maria:** she releases him when she needs another fighter; he survives because they cooperate. After the landing, returning to his old trade does not restore a viable harbor or crew. She is travelling toward the guarded supply route he already suspects matters. Initial common interest becomes trust through repeated shared risks, not instant repentance after captivity.

**Why descend instead of steal a boat:** he could try to flee. By the yard, however, he can recognize coastal cargo fittings reused on patient wagons and the destination of consignments he once robbed or carried. The route below is materially connected to the waters on which he expects to live. Stopping it protects his future and other crews; it also means acting on a connection he previously exploited.

Do not promise that ending the bargain immediately makes the sea safe. He is choosing a chance to change the cause over another temporary escape.

**What he knows at entry:** shipping marks, approaches, tide windows, the habits of Merfolk, and which vessels disappear. He may have carried sealed treatment cargo without knowing its biological effects.

**What he does NOT know:** the Blood Emblem's identity, the original expedition's full findings, Vael, the contract, or the Deep One's intentions. Fighting Merfolk teaches survival, not cosmology. Do not later reveal that he recognized Maria's emblem all along.

**Story through play:** a durable axe fighter is well suited to hold a boarding lane against spears. That existing interaction supports his experience without a new secret anti-aqua passive or guaranteed victory. His weak Resistance and ordinary limitations keep expertise local. Berserker advancement expresses a violent survivor, not proof he underwent blood treatment.

**Silent evidence:** old spear scoring on a boarding shield or a salvaged fitting; a patched coastal chart with avoided wreck sites; a cargo hook matching the yard; practiced use of a narrow gangway. New props and animations are proposals, not currently implemented advantages.

**Arc:** profit from dangerous routes -> recognize the routes as a system consuming people -> use hard-earned maritime knowledge to help break it. He can remain abrasive, practical and a pirate; trust need not turn him into a knight.

### Cyrus - Useful knowledge without the whole answer

**Existing foundation:** a Mage encountered in the fishing village, there for unspecified reasons. His Fire tome's lore includes tide tables and a repeatedly crossed-out name.

**Visual identity:** black hair; navy/cobalt field robes with ivory lining, brass edging and worn brown leather; an amber lantern at his belt. The lantern suggests Survey fieldwork without proving his whole background. Mage and Sage retain this outfit for now. Casting gestures contain no elemental projectile: Fire, Thunder and green Wind are separate reusable effects. His display name is Cyrus; the internal `cypher` identifier remains for save compatibility.

**Proposed background:** a former junior field worker for the Lantern Survey who compiled tide measurements and checked recovered materials. He worked on the surface logistics of an expedition, not the original chamber where the emblem was found. He returns to reconcile missing field records and a missing colleague with an official report that does not match his measurements.

**Faction:** former or estranged Survey associate, not its secret leader. The crossed-out name can belong to that colleague, but its identity and fate remain open.

**Why join Maria:** defending the village supplies immediate cause. Her route then overlaps the camp, city and requisition chain he was already investigating. She is willing to act where an official petition would merely reveal his location.

**Why stay and descend:** the Cathedral and yard show that research has become a working operation. Recovering a page or clearing his own name is no longer sufficient. Below, he can help recognize apparatus and discover where the measurements led, while the company can act on what he alone could only observe.

**Knowledge boundary:** he recognizes Survey practices and modern copies of instruments. He cannot translate every ancient inscription, identify every monster, know the giver, or announce the contract's rules. Technical literacy should sharpen observation, not replace exploration.

**Story through play:** choosing spells for beasts or aquatic enemies suggests practical study; Fire is not itself proof he knows every creature. A frail mage needs formation and protection, making investigation a cooperative undertaking.

**Silent evidence and friction:** reused Survey paper among spell exercises, a marked tide line matching a ruined quay, and an instrument case with a removed insignia. He initially values intact evidence where others value immediate destruction. Resolve that tension through what he preserves and what he helps disable, not a speech about scientific ethics.

**Arc:** recover an explanation -> confront the use of knowledge -> accept responsibility without claiming ownership of the truth.

### Seymour - Care is not the institution

**Existing foundation:** a Church Priest found among herbs in a refuge near the village. He has come from the city and joins as a staff healer. Prior copy makes his motives deliberately guarded.

**Proposed background:** a low-ranking parish worker sent to accompany treatment deliveries and assess coastal need. He witnessed real recoveries and initially trusted the Church's account. His work helped normalize the treatment without giving him access to laboratory protocols.

**Faction:** Healing Church, pastoral rather than initiated research authority. Do not automatically make him a Sanguine Covenant insider, the expedition survivor, or the person who gave Rictor the emblem.

**Why join Maria:** she helps people leave the village while official support has ceased. Travelling with her lets him continue caring for living people rather than guarding a deserted service post.

**Why stay and descend:** by the yard, he has evidence that people once entrusted to treatment are still being processed. Returning to a parish does not stop that harm. He goes to interrupt the supply and attend victims, not to recover his reputation or insist that the Church was blameless.

**Knowledge boundary:** routine treatment seals and delivery practices, not the ancient origin. Recognition of clerical insignia can be shown through shared visual motifs; he must not be required to explain the Blood Beast.

**Story through play:** his limited staff uses and need for protection make care tangible work. Staff healing remains distinct from injected healing blood. Becoming a Bishop grants tools of the office without requiring loyalty to Voss or an ecclesiastical promotion ceremony.

**Silent evidence and friction:** worn vestments repaired beside common clothes, ordinary herb supplies kept separate from sealed blood vessels, an institutional badge left face down while the staff remains in use. These are individual choices, not a declaration that all faith is false.

**Arc:** trust an authority that provides care -> recognize its concealment -> retain care while refusing its authority over victims.

### Stark - From seeking a cure to refusing another dose

**Existing foundation:** an island hunter encountered in the Order's camp. His sister is ill; he is heading toward Aldermere in hope of treatment. Her red scarf and his beast-effective Hunter Bow already provide personal and gameplay anchors.

**Visual continuity:** Stark keeps the red scarf in all three outfits. The map and battle sprites share the same class silhouettes: leather and steel for Archer, a split-tailed ivory coat for Sniper, and a dark cloth hood for Veilstalker. The supplied map sets have eight facings each, with eight walking frames and nine bow-attack frames per facing. Veilstalker's active map art lives in [Veilstalker_Stark](../assets/character/Veilstalker_Stark); the previous Covenant map art is archived.

**Proposed background:** a household provider whose work became progressively less viable as game changed and roads closed. He is skilled at hunting, not the member of a secret hunter order or a bloodline connected to the ancient city.

**Faction:** independent civilian hunter.

**Why join Maria:** she is already traversing the dangerous route to the city; his ranged hunting supports her fighters, while they make his own journey possible.

**Why stay after the Cathedral:** discovering that a promised cure harms people does not tell him how to help his sister. It does tell him that another official dose is not an answer he can safely bring home.

**Why descend:** he follows the actual origin of the treatment in search of a way to interrupt its effects, not because anyone promises a cure below. Even if no reversal exists, preventing more distribution protects households facing the same decision. Do not resolve his sister's condition offscreen merely to simplify this motivation.

**Knowledge boundary:** local tracks, hunting sites and animal changes. He cannot diagnose the whole plague from a footprint or know his sister's latest condition from a distance.

**Story through play:** beast-effective arrows suggest an adapted livelihood. Range-two combat and vulnerability at close quarters reward the cooperation he needs. New enemies can echo familiar anatomy without granting automatic identification.

**Silent evidence and friction:** keep the scarf through promotion; let abandoned household treatment kits resemble the one he intended to take back. His willingness to preserve a possible remedy should not make him casually indifferent to current victims.

**Arc:** obtain the advertised cure -> reject the false certainty attached to it -> seek help without demanding that other people pay its price.

### Linus - Duty after disobedience

**Existing foundation:** a wounded Cavalier defending villagers at the crossing against his captain's command. Maria must reach him to recruit him; he keeps his wounds. His service lance bears signs of retreat.

**Proposed background:** a junior Golden Order rider accustomed to escorting civilians along the road. He complied with early restrictions believing the boundary protected people on both sides. Being ordered to abandon those outside makes the contradiction concrete.

**Faction:** Golden Order, crossing detachment; a deserter from a command, not necessarily from the duty he understands himself to have.

**Why join Maria:** her actions at the village align with the people he is trying to protect. She supplies relief, not a persuasive argument that changes his mind without evidence.

**Why stay and descend:** the crossing is one endpoint of a system that will keep producing frightened, displaced people. By Chapter 16 he can see that the closed route and patient wagons belong to the same protected traffic. Helping stop it is a continuation of his original duty.

**Knowledge boundary:** checkpoint orders, formations and escort procedure. He is not a junior officer who somehow knows the entire conspiracy.

**Story through play:** durable cavalry can cover ground between vulnerable people; joining wounded records the cost of his refusal. A later Paladin masters swords, lances and axes without accepting Aurelan's or Severin's priorities. His personal Covenant alternative, Bloodsworn, keeps swords/lances but trades Axe access for stronger defensive promotion gains and HP-paid Blood Oath protection: Linus will go further to keep others safe.

**Silent evidence and friction:** his lance faces the beasts while the checkpoint line faces the village. Keep a repaired service crest instead of replacing all his history with pirate colors. He can work beside Bartz without pretending piracy and soldiering are morally interchangeable.

**Arc:** obey the protective boundary -> break formation for people -> choose what duty means when command is no longer credible.

### Rictor - Responsibility without secret omniscience

**Existing foundation:** Maria's husband, an Oathkeeper imprisoned in the quarantine ward after refusing the roundups. Lena releases him and recovers his Vigil Lance. Its filed crest matches Maria's Rapier. His Golden Order membership is established narrative direction; civic-watch service is compatible with that affiliation.

**Proposed background:** an Order member assigned to civic protection and occasional expedition security. A surviving Survey participant, serving as a Church liaison, trusted him with the recovered Blood Emblem after observing his refusal to surrender an injured worker. This remains the [proposed handoff](story-bible.md#6-rictor-maria-and-the-missing-expedition), not a newly finalized event.

**Faction:** Golden Order, estranged from its command. Do not silently classify every other city-watch ally as an Order member to match him.

**Why the party stays:** he takes responsibility in the ward and infirmary before being reunited with Maria. His competence and familiarity matter, but Lena, Arlan and Troy are not his unquestioning retainers.

**Why leave the emblem with Maria:** to keep an unregistered object out of expected searches. It was an act of trust and concealment, not preparation of a successor or a plan to draw her to the island. The omission put her at risk.

**Why descend after reunion:** escaping with Maria is desirable. The patient route and missing research remain active, however, and his earlier service helped protect parts of that system. He now has an opportunity to interrupt it with allies rather than merely disappear from it.

**Knowledge boundary:** he knew enough to distrust custody and hide the object, not enough to understand immortality or Vael's contract. He must not have knowingly sent Maria into a supernatural role.

**Story through play:** the Oathkeeper's spear and eventual Templar discipline express protection practised against his former command. Helping prisoners and holding the evacuation route establish credibility before any lore about his former service.

**Silent evidence and friction:** paired filed crests, confiscated belongings and recognizable escort fittings. Maria's discovery can change how they handle the emblem together without an explanatory confrontation. Avoid making his guilt the only reason he acts.

**Arc:** preserve an oath by withholding obedience -> discover how much his service protected -> accept a shared decision rather than deciding alone what Maria may know.

### Lena - Open the door, then stop the cages

**Existing foundation:** a Thief who owes Rictor her life, breaks him out, retrieves his lance, and opens locks or steals carried items through actual gameplay.

**Proposed background:** an urban thief and occasional smuggler of people past checkpoints. Rictor previously refused to hand her over to a punitive detention detail. Their relationship begins with a specific mercy, not secret romance, kinship or an all-encompassing debt.

**Faction:** independent; an informal network of safe houses is plausible, but no new named thieves' guild is required.

**Why join Rictor:** she came to free a person who once protected her. The ward turns a private rescue into direct evidence of people being held and relabeled.

**Why stay and descend:** opening one cell cannot stop the intake of prisoners. At the yard she sees where the locked transport goes. She follows to destroy the mechanism that makes people vanish, not to repay a debt indefinitely.

**Knowledge boundary:** locks, service access, urban routes and what can be carried away. She can recognize a tampered seal without decoding an ancient religion.

**Story through play:** retrieving a staff with its remaining uses removes a captor's real capability; theft is intervention, not just treasure collection. Assassin promotion describes combat specialization, not membership in an unseen assassination order.

**Silent evidence and friction:** varied reused picks, opened domestic locks contrasted with standardized institutional ones, and personal belongings left outside numbered cells. She distrusts seals and authorization that Linus or Rictor once respected. Her expertise can make escape possible, which makes choosing descent meaningful.

**Arc:** rescue one creditor -> act for people not personally owed anything -> attack the infrastructure of captivity.

### Arlan - The doorway is no longer enough

**Existing foundation:** a city-watch Knight defending the infirmary's evacuation route. High Defense, low mobility and Ridersbane make him suited to holding a narrow entrance against cavalry.

**Proposed background:** a long-serving civic guard assigned to protect staff and patients. Early custody orders looked like an extension of that work. He did not author them, but he held a door that kept people inside before he held one to get them out.

**Faction:** city watch, operating under Church/Order emergency authority. Membership in the Golden Order is not yet established and need not be added.

**Why join Rictor:** a recognized fellow protector is physically helping evacuate the people Arlan was posted to defend. The relationship is reinforced by a common task, not just a past acquaintance.

**Why stay and descend:** completing the evacuation saves one group. Freight evidence shows others are being carried beyond any surface doorway he can hold. He goes because protection must follow them rather than remain attached to his old post.

**Knowledge boundary:** ward procedures, guard assignments and patient markings. He can recognize an issued gown or rider formation, not know the entire contract history.

**Story through play:** slow movement imposes the cost of carrying protection forward. Anti-cavalry equipment tells the history of stopping enforcers at a door. General advancement should keep the shield battered, not turn him into Severin with different allegiance.

**Silent evidence and friction:** dents on the inward-facing shield edge and a ward duty marker retained among personal belongings. He values holding a defensible position; Troy values reaching people beyond it. Both approaches can be useful without a scripted quarrel.

**Arc:** guard the assigned place -> protect the people leaving it -> move beyond the place when the harm has moved.

### Troy - A route is never neutral

**Existing foundation:** a city-watch Cavalier familiar to Arlan, joining in Chapter 13 with swords, spears and a Javelin. He knows the old watch route. His profile emphasizes speed more than Linus's durability.

**Proposed background:** a patrol and dispatch rider who relayed changes in gates, escorts and delivery schedules. He carried sealed orders without authoring them. He kept trying to maintain connections between districts after command began using the same network to isolate them.

**Faction:** city watch; unlike Linus, not automatically a Golden Order field rider.

**Why join Rictor:** Arlan supplies an existing connection, and the escaped ward party gives him a concrete route worth opening. He can help someone use the streets rather than simply deliver another restriction along them.

**Why stay and descend:** the yard reveals that the routes he served do not end at the Cathedral. He recognizes dispatch hardware and route conventions continuing toward the lift. Turning away would leave the network functioning, with a different rider carrying the next order.

**Knowledge boundary:** current streets, signals and transfers. He does not know the underground layout because he has ridden above it, and he cannot locate every surviving civilian by intuition.

**Story through play:** varied carried weapons and high movement express a responder who needs to handle different situations, not a stronger copy of Linus. His speed can reach a threatened healer where Arlan cannot.

**Silent evidence and friction:** changed seals on an old route token, polished saddle wear and a dispatch tube whose contents are not a full plot summary. His early practical loyalty is to colleagues and access; descent turns that into responsibility for where access leads.

**Arc:** keep the routes working -> recognize what they move -> redirect or interrupt the network deliberately.

### Eveline - Helping without another false promise

**Existing foundation:** a mounted healer treating survivors at the shuttered apothecary. Rictor alone can recruit her. She knows Corvis from ward service and can recruit him later. She can be lost, and the campaign continues without her.

**Proposed background:** a field practitioner employed by the Church's treatment service, moving between wards and homes. She administered approved regimens before observing repeated deterioration. She is neither the mastermind of the trials nor an outsider with no responsibility for them.

**Faction:** Church-associated clinical worker; initiation into the Sanguine Covenant is not established.

**Why join Rictor:** she has remained with patients when official service withdrew. His party is visibly protecting people rather than collecting them. Recommended justification for his exclusive recruitment: she recognizes his ward detail and sees the same protector assisting survivors. The exact action can be staged silently; no mechanical change to the required recruiter is assumed.

**Why stay and descend:** surviving patients may be exposed again, while other patients are still in transit. She goes to interrupt the source of repeat harm and help those encountered, not because she expects to find a vial that repairs every treatment she gave.

**Knowledge boundary:** dose practices, symptoms she has seen and colleagues she served beside. That is not knowledge of ancient medicine or a diagnosis of every monster.

**Story through play:** mounted healing reaches people whom a stationary institution left behind. Her fragility and vulnerability before recruitment make her refusal to leave a real risk. Promotion to Valkyrie broadens her ability to intervene, not her Church rank.

**Silent evidence and friction:** emptied ordinary supply pockets beside unopened treatment fittings; a familiar ward clasp seen again on Corvis. She may prioritize a living patient over an intact specimen. Neither her personal regret nor a future contract decision authorizes her to decide for all victims.

**Arc:** apply the approved treatment -> recognize its repeated harms -> practise care without promising certainty she does not possess.

### Corvis - Partial complicity, practical refusal

**Existing foundation:** a Shaman guarding the Cathedral courtyard, previously assigned to the ward. Eveline alone recruits him; he keeps his existing HP and tome uses. He can remain an opponent if she is absent. Dark Sage advancement adds Anima and staff use alongside Dark, and doubles spell and staff capacity.

**Proposed background:** a junior Sanguine Covenant technician assigned to catalogue treatment reactions and maintain ward apparatus. His access was compartmentalized. He recorded failures under approved categories and accepted the claim that containment protected others, even when he had enough reason to question it.

**Faction:** Church defender and proposed Covenant subordinate, not a senior author of the experiments.

**Why join Rictor's party:** not because an enemy leader wins an argument. Eveline is a living colleague contradicting the official account of the ward; her presence and treatment equipment make the human consequences recognizable. His lowered weapon is an action against the line he was guarding.

**Why stay and descend:** after the Cathedral, his technical work is still being reproduced in the shipment apparatus. He can help identify and interrupt modern systems derived from the old ones. Contributing to that task is more substantial than surrendering and declaring himself absolved.

**Knowledge boundary:** modern protocols, notation and fittings. He can compare a copy to an older original, not automatically read the original. He did not attend Vael's bargain, cannot explain the Deep One, and is not the missing expedition survivor by default.

**Story through play:** dark magic's draining tools echo the treatment system without making all dark spells evil. A promoted Dark Sage can use a staff to support the people once detained by his institution. Capacity is a real class feature, not proof of ancient ancestry.

**Silent evidence and friction:** a technician's clasp fitted to a guard uniform; catalogue marks matching those on ward fittings; the same hand used to tend an enemy apparatus later disabling one. Lena has good reasons not to trust his credentials. A useful act does not erase his prior choices.

**Arc:** classify suffering at a distance -> recognize people behind the categories -> use the tools against the practice, without demanding forgiveness.

Seymour and Eveline are also different: one represents pastoral trust and community service; the other hands-on clinical participation. None needs to be a secret villain to create friction.

## 7. Implementation boundaries and presentation backlog

1. **Bartz's expertise is already supported, not mechanically unique.** Axe advantage against spears is a shared rule. Do not claim a Merfolk-only damage bonus, knowledge passive or bespoke animation exists.
2. **Weapon lore is not an ownership registry.** Shared inventory entries can move between characters. A steel lance's Linus-specific lore does not make Troy's copy evidence of theft or a personal relationship. Future personal evidence may need distinct presentation, not a global rewrite that assigns every copy to one person.
3. **Keep the three emblem identities separate.** A paired weapon crest shows Maria and Rictor's history; it must not make an Order crest identical to the Blood Emblem. None of the twelve is automatically initiated into the ancient bargain.
4. **Retain present recruitment logic until deliberately revised.** Maria reaches Linus; Rictor recruits Eveline; Eveline recruits Corvis. Replace dialogue with clear silent actions, not invisible triggers. Preserve current HP, spent uses, recruitment risks and optionality.
5. **Optional allies cannot gate understanding or completion.** If Eveline is absent, Corvis may remain an opponent. Survey markings, clinical evidence and the descent's purpose must still be legible without them. Personal arcs enrich the route; they do not supply a mandatory missing key.
6. **No mandatory character biographies in item slots.** Charts, scarves, badges and clutches of tools are proposed visual/personal evidence, not automatically new inventory burdens or equippable stat items.
7. **No secret infection or ancestry reveals to justify loyalty.** Such a change requires an explicit future decision. Practical familiarity is more useful than making every ally another owner of the truth.
8. **No promised universal cure.** Stark's hope, Seymour's faith and Eveline's responsibility do not settle the contract's final consequences. Ancient victims' release and surface treatment reversal remain distinct questions.
9. **Respect tactical control.** Character motives should be visible in authored entry positions, recruitment and short staging, not force the player to choose a particular unit for every thematic task.
10. **Preserve uncertainty without making everyone obtuse.** Once patients and freight visibly match, characters should act on it. Silence is a presentation choice, not an inability to recognize evidence.

## 8. Decisions to approve later

- Bartz's former freight work, how directly he participated in treatment shipping, and what remains of his crew. Do not assert that every original boarder was killed or willingly follows him afterward.
- Cyrus's Survey employment and the identity behind the crossed-out name. He should not become the emblem giver by convenience.
- Seymour's precise Church assignment and whether he personally administered blood, as distinct from staff or ordinary medical care.
- Stark's sister's location, condition and eventual outcome.
- Linus's rank and service history beyond the crossing.
- The event behind Lena's debt and whether an escape network needs visible supporting people.
- Arlan and Troy's relationship to Order command; civic-watch service does not settle formal membership.
- Eveline's treatment responsibilities and the visual cue that makes Rictor her trusted recruiter.
- Corvis's exact junior Covenant role and limits of access.
- Whether any personal evidence is a named keepsake, a sprite detail or environmental art. Prefer a few strong clues to twelve sets of collectible backstory.
- How the final contract decision affects living patients, ancient subjects and each character's hoped-for outcome.
