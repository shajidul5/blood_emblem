# Blood-Emblem

A starter project for an HD-2D style game: crisp pixel-art sprites standing in a lit, shadowed 3D world, built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) and [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/).

Infantry and armored units share one visible idle height, including promoted sprites such as Bishop Seymour. Mounted units are 20% taller; monsters retain their authored sizes. Sizing measures visible artwork rather than transparent animation padding, preserves each animation's proportions and keeps feet grounded without resampling the source art. Existing saves use the corrected sizing automatically.

## Chapter loading

The title screen does not fetch or build Chapter 1. **Continue** restores only the saved map; **New Game** loads the starting map on demand. A blocking chapter-loading overlay covers New Game, Continue, save-slot loading, chapter restart, chapter advance, and imported maps. Its progress follows artwork loading, unique deployed-unit animation sets, and time-budgeted texture uploads; shaders and a first render are prepared before gameplay is revealed. Uploads are cached per renderer and refreshed when textures change or are disposed. The tactical loop pauses during preparation and on the title screen. Idle poses remain available for roster previews and future spawns; only fielded motion is required before play, with upcoming reinforcement motion and uploads queued sequentially afterward. Sprite normalization yields between individual frames so background preparation does not monopolize the main thread. Missing artwork or animations is reported on the title screen, and a failed Continue does not overwrite the saved game.

## Character animation gallery

Choose **Character Compendium** from Options on the title screen, in preparations or during play, or open [character-gallery.html](character-gallery.html) through the dev server. It opens in a new tab, leaving the current game and saves untouched. The compendium covers all 14 playable characters, their implemented Orders/Covenant promotions, eight-direction map clips, battle sheets, spell effects and class abilities. A separate **Adversaries** dropdown group includes the Undead Prisoner, Rotten, generic Paladin, General and Sage without adding them to the playable roster. Each available battle set can be tried with the actual renderer, including criticals, ranged weapons, mirrored attacks and support casting. Missing dedicated battle art is explicitly labeled. Gallery loops can be paused and honor reduced-motion preferences; no save storage is accessed.

Battle sprites use authored head/body landmarks rather than weapon tips or transparent sheet padding to normalize ordinary allies and enemies to the same 130px standing body height. Mounted sets share a slightly taller 140px rider-to-hoof height. Idle, attacks, criticals, throws, staff casts and compendium previews use the same scale; melee contacts remain aligned. Human bosses retain a 12% size increase, while monsters retain their authored larger silhouettes. Reload applies this presentation-only change without restarting chapters or changing saves.

For a single self-contained HTML file that opens offline, run `npm run character-gallery` (writes `dist/character-gallery-standalone.html`), or `npm run character-gallery -- "C:\path\to\gallery.html"` to choose the destination. The export embeds the original art and current runtime, so rerun it after adding art or changing class definitions. Preview equipment includes class-compatible spells irrespective of campaign unlocks; displayed combat damage is illustrative.

On Windows, if a production build reports `EMFILE` while loading thousands of sprite frames, run `$env:UV_THREADPOOL_SIZE = '64'; npm.cmd run build` in PowerShell to increase the filesystem worker pool.

- **Veilstalker:** Bow specialist with 6 base Movement. **Blood Trail** grants +4 immediately while any living wounded enemy is within 10 tiles (Manhattan distance, ignoring intervening terrain). Both classes use the approved map/walking sprites and portraits; Bloodsworn also uses his protective casting clip. Map combat uses the existing fallback where dedicated directional attack frames are unavailable. Saves preserve unspent Blood Oath protection; Blood Trail is calculated from the current battlefield rather than stored. See [the class designs](docs/story-bible.md#covenant-branch-art-direction---bloodsworn-and-veilstalker) for their thematic significance.

## Map controls on desktop and phones

Tap/click units and tiles as before. Drag the map with a finger, pen or the primary mouse button to pan without moving the cursor or issuing an order; small tap jitter is ignored. The camera remains inside the chapter's existing bounds. Tapping a tile, moving the keyboard cursor or choosing **Next unit** resumes cursor following; movement, enemy phases and scripted scenes retain their automatic camera.

The top-right **Map menu** hamburger holds enemy ranges, Options, map import and the editor. It closes after choosing a tool, clicking outside, or pressing Escape. Bottom-right **Next unit** cycles living allies who have not acted; finish or cancel a selected unit first. **Back** cancels targeting, undoes an uncommitted move or returns from Preparations' map preview. Neither control is active during combat, enemy phases or modal dialogs.

Phone layouts support portrait and landscape, safe-area insets, 44px-or-larger controls and a scrollable contextual action sheet. Secondary tile/experience details and keyboard hints are hidden on compact screens to leave the map clear; profiles, Options and reward/experience notifications remain available. No virtual joystick or extra confirmation overlay is required.

## Side-view combat animations

Forest Environmental Sounds use generated natural crow recordings, not oscillator beeps, over canopy wind and rustling leaves. The first crow phrase plays 0.6–1 second after its audio loads, then every 8–14 seconds at a stronger mix. Muting, leaving the forest or hiding the page cancels the scheduler. ## Controls

The game uses turn-based tactical controls:

- Arrow keys or WASD move the battlefield cursor one tile at a time.
- Press **Tab** to cycle through available player units; the current roster has Maria.
- Enter or Space selects Maria or confirms a movement destination / attack target.
- Click Maria to select her, then click a reachable tile or in-range enemy to confirm; menu actions are also clickable.
- Selecting Maria immediately opens her movement range; choose a reachable tile to move, or press Escape to skip movement and open her actions.
- While choosing a movement destination, a thick blue route line with a direction tip shows the path through each tile.
- Enemy ranges pinned with **Space** stay visible while a unit is selected and moving; during movement every tile the pinned enemies can reach or strike is shown in red beneath the blue movement tiles.
- **Convoy** appears in the action menu for Maria, Rictor, and any unit standing next to either of them. It opens the inventory with the shared convoy's Store and Take controls and does not use the unit's action.
- While choosing a movement tile, click Maria to leave movement targeting and open her action menu.
- After moving, choose **Back · Undo Move** from Maria's action menu to return to her starting tile and select a new destination. The Back button keeps its normal cancel behavior when undo is unavailable. Undo is available until Maria attacks or ends her turn, and is preserved when continuing a saved game.
- Press **Backspace** to activate the contextual Back action from the keyboard; **Escape** also backs out of movement or attack targeting.
- Tap either **Ctrl** key (without another shortcut key) or **right-click** for universal Back. In Preparations, these return every subview, including Trade and Convoy, to its parent; nested Profile/Options dialogs close first, and View Map returns to Preparations. Back at the preparation hub keeps it open.
- **Restart Chapter** after leaving Preparations keeps everything done there: deployment, trades, convoy changes, used items, promotions and Insight purchases. Preparations reopen with those choices so you can adjust them. Starting items are not granted twice.
- In Preparations, select a unit under **Inventory**, then **Convoy** to use an Orders Emblem directly from storage, even with a full inventory. Carried emblems work under **Use Item**. Promotion requires level 20 and a base class; it consumes one emblem without using the unit's battle action.
- After movement, use the arrow keys or WASD to move focus through the action menu and Space or Enter to activate the focused action.
- The attack picker lists the equipped weapon or spell first. Choosing an attack weapon equips it immediately, including its matching sprite, and keeps it equipped for counters and saves.
- Choose **Profile** from Maria's action menu to view her base/current stats, growth rates, level, experience, and equipped weapon.
- Choose **Inventory** from Maria's action menu to equip her Iron Sword or Rapier, or use her three-use Potion. The Rapier has 6 Might, 85 Hit, and 10 Critical; the Potion restores up to 10 HP per use and disappears after its third use. Using an item ends Maria's unit turn, while equipping a weapon does not.
- Inspect an enemy by clicking it or moving the cursor onto it and pressing Enter to view its stats and equipped weapon.
- Maria's class-based movement range is 5 tiles for her Myrmidon class.
- Select **Attack** to target enemies within her weapon range.
- Confirming a target opens a combat forecast with each side's damage per hit, predicted attack count, Hit, and Critical rates; confirm or cancel before combat begins.
- Arrow keys or WASD navigate buttons in menus and dialogs, including the combat forecast.
- Hover over a map tile to see its terrain type, whether it is walkable, and its combat bonuses.
- **End Unit** (or `F`) finishes Maria's actions; once every player unit has finished, enemies take their turn. **End Phase** (or `E`) ends the player phase early.
- Escape cancels the current targeting mode or deselects Maria.
- The main menu offers **New Game** and **Continue**. Progress auto-saves in this browser; clicking an unoccupied tile or choosing **Options** opens a menu with **Return to Main Menu**.
- In Preparations, **Use Item** lists each item's lore; Trade and Convoy screens hide lore to keep the two columns compact. **Insight** shows item sprites and leadership stars. Each leadership star gives the party +3 Hit and +3 Avoid, costing 4/6/8/10/12 Insight; refunds return the price actually paid (older saves' stars refund at the original 2–6 prices). Maria's Blood Emblem awakens in three tiers, bought in order: **Blood Aura** (20 Insight) heals Maria and orthogonally adjacent allies 10 HP at the start of each player phase. **Ravage** (40, opens after Chapter 6) adds a command that costs 20% of her max HP for +5 Might until your next turn, counters included. ## Character and weapon data

Character profiles live in `data/characters/` and store base stats, current stats, growth rates (percentages), level, experience, and an equipped weapon ID. Weapon data lives in `data/weapons/` and includes might, weight, hit, critical, and range. Consumable data lives in `data/items/`; playable level data lives in `data/levels/`. Animation metadata is stored in `data/character-assets/`, while sprite images and other visual files remain in `assets/`. Maria starts at level 1 with 0 EXP, matching base/current stats, an Iron Sword, a Rapier, and a three-use Potion; her HP, inventory uses, and equipped weapon are tracked by the battle state and save.

Combat uses strength + weapon might − the defender's defense for physical weapons (magic and resistance for magical weapons). Attack speed is speed − max(0, weapon weight − build): build only cancels out weapon weight and never raises attack speed above speed. A unit doubles when its attack speed is at least 4 higher than its opponent's.

Physical reavers reverse **both** triangle matchups and double their damage, Hit and Avoid modifiers, not total attack damage. Axereaver (lance) gains double advantage against axes and double disadvantage against swords; Lancereaver (sword) gains double advantage against lances and double disadvantage against axes. Same-type ordinary weapons, bows, guns and physical-versus-magic matchups remain neutral. Two opposing reavers cancel the reversal and use the ordinary triangle.

The separate **magic triangle** is **Anima > Holy (Light) > Dark > Anima**. Advantage gives +1 damage, +10 Hit and +10 Avoid; disadvantage applies matching penalties, just like the physical triangle. Fire, Thunder and Wind share Anima and are neutral against each other. Matching schools, staves and physical-versus-magic matchups remain neutral; physical reavers never affect magic. Forecast arrows, enemy target evaluation, attacks and counters use the same shared rules. Holy magic uses the existing `Light` weapon type; no equipment or save conversion is needed.

The polearm weapon rank is displayed as **Lance**, including profiles, rank-up notifications and equipment requirements. The internal `Spear` EXP/type key remains unchanged so older saves keep all progress. **Spear** is a distinct heavy range 1-2 weapon, not the name of the rank.

The drowned civilization's four **ancient relics** are reliable rank-B physical weapons with unlimited uses, zero weapon Crit and no special effects. They are not a silver equipment set; killer weapons retain their 30-Crit niche. Bows retain the ordinary flying weakness matchup.

| Relic (item ID) | Type | Mt | Wt | Hit | Range |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starless Edge (`starless-edge`) | Sword | 13 | 8 | 85 | 1 |
| Tidepiercer (`tidepiercer`) | Spear | 14 | 10 | 80 | 1 |
| Keelbreaker (`keelbreaker`) | Axe | 16 | 12 | 75 | 1 |
| Horizon String (`horizon-string`) | Bow | 12 | 8 | 85 | 2 |

These relics and Evelynn (`evelynn`) have custom 64×64 PixelLab-generated inventory icons in `assets/weapons/Ancient_Relics/`; class weapon access, rank checks, inventory, convoy, and equipment menus use their normal shared mechanisms.

Displayed Hit is weapon Hit + 2×Dexterity + floor(Luck/2) − enemy Avoid, clamped to 0–100. Avoid is 2×Attack Speed + Luck + terrain Avoid. Each strike uses two random rolls averaged together (2RN). Boat terrain is neutral; the walkable Cover tile grants +20 Avoid and +1 physical Defense to its occupant.

Hovering a tile shows whether it is walkable and its terrain bonuses. Stairs grant +10 Avoid, legacy Quarterdeck tiles grant +10 Avoid and +1 Defense, and legacy Helm tiles grant +20 Avoid and +1 Defense. Bartz's Defense has been reduced from 7 to 5.

Each attack plays its directional katana animation; follow-up strikes pause for one second, and combat temporarily locks tactical input.

Critical rate follows the GBA-style formula: weapon Critical + floor(attacker Dexterity/2) − defender Luck, clamped to 0–100. A successful hit gets a separate one-roll critical check. Critical damage triples the damage after defense is subtracted; ordinary and critical hits use yellow and dark-red floating damage numbers, while misses show `MISS`.

Combat EXP is awarded once at the end of the exchange, including counterattacks. Base enemies grant 10 EXP if they survive or 30 when defeated, with +40 for a boss kill. Advanced-class enemies grant 15/100 EXP; advanced bosses also grant 100 base kill EXP. Each enemy level above the gaining unit adds 10% of the base reward, **without an upper cap**; each level below subtracts 10%, down to a 10% floor. A level-2 unit defeating a level-12 base enemy earns **60 EXP**, not the former 45 ceiling. Fixed healing and objective awards are unchanged. At each 100 EXP the unit levels up, retaining all overflow and processing every crossed level's growth rolls. The EXP bar animates the full reward and all level-up rollovers.
